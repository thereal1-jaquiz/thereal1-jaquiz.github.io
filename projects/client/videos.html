Cool Songs and Videos about Beavers!

The video above is made by National Geographic and goes over the life of beavers as well as a few fun facts about them. The video shows what they do, how it benifits the ecosystem, and what adaptations they have that aid them in thier day to day experiences out in the wild.

In this short video, a ranger from Acadia National Park presents some of the physical adaptations of beavers and describes why they are useful to their aquatic lifestyle.

This lengthier video features the role of beavers as a keystone species whose incredible engineering skills in creating habitats are essential to supporting biodiversity and naturally managing water.

In this super cute video from Animal Wonders Montana we meet a resued beaver kit. The video shows and describes how they take care of the young kit and why he was in need of help

"I'm Proud to be a Beaver" by the Banana Slug String Band is a fun and catchy song about beavers.

This video shows Camp Barambas version of the Beaver Song and their accompanying dance.
